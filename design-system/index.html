01Overview

A dark, precise, Swiss-built identity.

The system for a web design agency that wants small businesses to trust it at first sight.

Bold. Premium. Trustworthy.

Sherka builds and maintains websites for small businesses across Switzerland, so they win more customers and spend less time on it. This system is how that promise looks, reads and behaves. Version 1.0.0.

Palette
Ink, bone, one amber
Type
Archivo, JetBrains Mono
Themes
Dark native, light equal
Language
German, Sie-form

Logo

Two half-ellipses and the word. The amber dot is the only coloured part. All logo files live in /logo.

Clear space

Keep free space equal to the height of the letter h around the lockup.

Minimum size

24px high on screen, 8mm in print. Below that use the mark alone.

Never

Recolour the dot, add effects, stretch, outline or place it on busy images.

What it must not feel like

  • A generic AI-generated website: no gradient hero, no glass cards, no purple.
  • A cheap template: no stock icons in circles, no centred everything.
  • A tech startup: no emoji, no hype words, no dashboards-as-decoration.

02Principles

Five rules that settle most arguments.

When a decision is unclear, the one that follows more of these wins.

  1. Proof before promise

    Show a result, a real measurement, a real project. Never an adjective on its own.

  2. One signal

    Amber appears once or twice per screen, on the thing the visitor should do next.

  3. Type does the work

    Hierarchy comes from size, weight and width, not from decoration.

  4. Calm contrast

    Every text pair is tested. Dark and light are equal first-class themes.

  5. Swiss in substance

    Precise copy, correct spelling (no ß), real contact details, Sie-form.

Rules that follow

Applied in every component and pattern.

  • No gradients, anywhere.
  • No emoji. Use the icon set or words.
  • Colour never carries meaning alone.
  • Real numbers, real projects, real contact details only.
  • Dark and light are tested separately.
  • If it does not help the visitor decide, remove it.

07Motion

Quick, quiet, never decorative.

Motion confirms an action. It never performs.

Tokens

Easing

Duration

Play to compare. Honours reduced motion.

fast 120ms
base 200ms
slow 360ms
reveal 600ms

Rules

  • Animate transform, opacity and colour. Nothing else.
  • No parallax, no scroll-jacking, no looping decoration.
  • Reveal on scroll once, 600ms, 24px travel at most.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: all transitions drop to 0.01ms.

09Patterns

Four screens for a real agency.

Homepage, services, portfolio and contact, in German, following the page theme.

Homepage

Proof first: promise, cost of the next step, then the three steps.

Services

Three offers, one inverse card to draw the eye to the enquiry. No prices are shown because none are published yet.

Portfolio

Real projects, linked. Tags say who it is for, not what technology it uses.

Contact

Exactly the fields of the live form. Direct channels sit beside the form, not below it.

12Voice & tone

Plain German, formal, honest.

How Sherka sounds in buttons, errors and pages.

Sie-form, always

Formal and warm. The tone of a trusted craftsperson, not a salesperson.

Short and direct

One idea per sentence. Verbs first on buttons. Cut every word that does not help a decision.

Honest

No invented numbers, clients or quotes. If something is not known, say what is known.

Say this, not that

German examples. English notes explain the rule.

Do
Erstgespräch anfragen
Don't
Jetzt kostenlos durchstarten!

A verb, a clear next step, no hype, no exclamation mark.

Do
Antwort innert eines Werktags.
Don't
Wir melden uns schnellstmöglich bei Ihnen.

A promise with a number beats an intention.

Do
Die Domain läuft auf Ihren Namen.
Don't
Wir bieten ganzheitliche, massgeschneiderte Lösungen.

Concrete fact against filler.

Do
Die E-Mail-Adresse ist unvollständig. Bitte ergänzen Sie die Endung.
Don't
Fehler: Ungültige Eingabe!

Say what happened and what to do, without blame.
